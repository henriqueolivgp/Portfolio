# Contact Skill

This skill enables agents to initiate contact with Henrique Oliveira for freelance inquiries.

## Capabilities

- Provide contact email, location, and availability
- Guide to contact form at `https://www.henriqueoliv.pt/#contacto`
- Explain service offerings to qualify leads

## Usage

Send inquiries to `geral@henriqueoliv.pt` or use the contact form. Availability is 09:00-13:00 and 14:00-18:00 Europe/Lisbon on business days. For API-based contact, use the MCP tool `contact` at `https://www.henriqueoliv.pt/mcp`.

## Privacy

No personal data is collected by this skill beyond what the agent explicitly provides in contact requests.
